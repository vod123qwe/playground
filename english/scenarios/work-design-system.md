# 🗺️ Scene: Present a Design System to a client

**Mode:** 🎙️ role-play · **Skill:** Register + Fluency · **Difficulty:** Normal → Nightmare

## Setup
You're presenting your team's design system to a new foreign client. They are not
designers — they care about consistency, speed, and cost. You have ~3 minutes.

## 🎧 Input (model — listen first)
> "Thanks everyone. Let me walk you through the design system we've built. At its
> core, it's a single source of truth for our UI — components, tokens, and patterns
> that every product team reuses. The rationale behind this is simple: instead of
> rebuilding a button five times, we build it once, and any change propagates
> everywhere. What this means in practice is faster delivery and a consistent
> experience for your users. Let me zoom in on tokens first…"

## 🎙️ Your turn (4/3/2)
Present the system in your own words. Round 1: 4 min. Round 2: 3 min. Round 3: 2 min.
Aim for fewer pauses each round — not more words.

## Target chunks to use
- "Let me walk you through…"
- "a single source of truth"
- "The rationale behind this is…"
- "What this means in practice is…"

## 🔥 Difficulty scaling
- **Normal:** friendly client, no interruptions.
- **Nightmare:** client interrupts: *"Why not just use an off-the-shelf UI kit?"*
- **Hell:** skeptical CFO: *"This sounds expensive. What's the ROI?"*
