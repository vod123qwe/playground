# 🎮 Warm-up Scene: CS2 strat call (passion topic, low stakes)

**Mode:** 🎙️ role-play or 🎧 shadowing · **Skill:** Fluency · **Difficulty:** Normal

Lżejszy bieg na rozgrzewkę. Temat, który lubisz → mówisz odważniej, budujesz
płynność bez stresu pracy. Te same mechanizmy (4/3/2, chunki), niższa stawka.

## Setup
You're the in-game leader explaining the round strategy to your team before it
starts. Fast, confident, clear. ~60–90 seconds.

## 🎧 Input (model)
> "Okay, default to start, we take map control mid and watch for the early push.
> If they don't contest, we fake B and rotate fast to A — I want utility saved for
> the after-plant. On my call, not before. Comms clean, only callouts that matter."

## 🎙️ Your turn (4/3/2)
Call a strat for your favorite map. Doesn't have to be realistic — focus on
speaking fast and without long pauses.

## Target chunks (transferują się do pracy!)
- "Let's default to…" → ~ "Let's start with…" (meetings)
- "On my call" → ~ "When I give the signal"
- "Only callouts that matter" → ~ "Let's keep it to the essentials"

> Cel rozgrzewki: rozpędzić aparat mowy na temacie bez presji, zanim wejdziemy w scenę z pracy.
