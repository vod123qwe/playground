Anatomy and sizes

Row
label left (13 px, 78% ink), value right (tabular figures, 50% ink), control under both; 34 px minimum, 6 px between rows
Track
4 px, round ends, filled part in ink, the rest 12%
Thumb
18 px white circle, 1 px ring; hover adds a 5 px halo, press grows it 12%
Hit area
the whole 24 px tall track (WCAG 2.2 · 2.5.8 target size)
Default
a 2 × 10 px notch on the track, hidden when the value sits on it
Value field
looks like text; hover shows a field, focus a 1.5 px ring; the unit stays outside
Choices
sharp chips, 32 px, the chosen one filled in ink
Check
18 px square, fills in ink with a white tick
Groups
semibold title that folds (0.4 s ease-out, no spring), full-width dividers

Behaviour

  • Keyboard on the slider: ← → step, Page Up/Down × 10, Home / End (native range)
  • Number: click and type; Enter applies, Esc cancels, ↑ ↓ step, Shift × 10
  • Drag the label to scrub the whole range in about 240 px; hold Alt for fine steps
  • Double-click the slider or its label: back to the default
  • Screen readers hear the value with its unit (aria-valuetext)
  • Light and dark from the same tokens; reduced motion turns the animations off
